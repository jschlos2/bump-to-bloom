Fever

A fever is usually a sign your baby's immune system is fighting an infection. How concerning it is depends heavily on your baby's age.

Usually manageable at home

  • Dress baby lightly and keep the room comfortably cool
  • Offer breast milk, formula, or water frequently to prevent dehydration
  • A lukewarm (not cold) sponge bath can help baby feel more comfortable

Call your pediatrician if

  • Fever lasts more than 24 hours in a baby under 2, or more than 3 days in an older baby
  • Fever comes with fussiness, poor feeding, or fewer wet diapers than usual
  • You're simply unsure and want reassurance — that's always a good reason to call

Seek emergency care if

  • Trouble breathing, blue/gray skin, or a seizure — call 911
